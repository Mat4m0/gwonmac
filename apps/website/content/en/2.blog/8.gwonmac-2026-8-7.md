Restores GWonMac after the latest Guild Wars update and makes future client updates less disruptive. Parts of Build Management still have problems.

- **Compatibility:** GWonMac works again with the current Guild Wars client.
- **Future updates:** the features that are safe to keep running now keep working after more kinds of Guild Wars updates.
- **Templates:** the app now says more clearly when templates become available after you sign in.
- **App icon:** GWonMac has a new icon.

## Known issues

- Parts of the new Build Management tools still have problems. A fix needs more time.
