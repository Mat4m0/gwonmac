I honestly didn't expect to be writing this, but Guild Wars is now running flawlessly on my M1 Pro MacBook Pro with 16 GB of RAM. I'm getting a constant 60 FPS on a 4K display with the highest graphics settings.

It's also surprisingly efficient. It uses around 70% of a single CPU core on average and about 500 MB of active memory, with no thermal or performance warnings.

## How it works

GWonMac runs ArenaNet's official WebAssembly client inside a dedicated macOS app. It does not bundle or modify the game — the official client and game files are downloaded directly from ArenaNet, and you sign in with your own account.

Huge credit goes to [Jon / 3vcloud](https://github.com/3vcloud). He figured out how to get ArenaNet's WASM build running in a browser and shared the original proof of concept. Without his work, this version wouldn't exist. I took his approach and developed it into a polished, standalone Mac app.

## Looking for testers

This is still an **alpha version**, so I'm looking for people who are willing to test it on different Apple Silicon Macs. Please let me know how it performs, whether you find any bugs, and what could be improved.

You can download the app from the [releases page](https://github.com/Mat4m0/gwonmac/releases/latest) and share feedback on [Discord](https://discord.gg/Z9ft52RBD3), in the [Reddit thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/GuildWars/comments/1v4sehu/guild_wars_native_experience_achieved_on_apple/), or through the [GitHub bug form](https://github.com/Mat4m0/gwonmac/issues/new?template=bug-report.yml).

The project is free, [open source](https://github.com/Mat4m0/gwonmac/), and independent. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by ArenaNet or NCSoft.

As far as I know, this is currently the closest thing to a native Guild Wars experience on Apple Silicon. Feel free to try it and let me know how it runs on your Mac.
