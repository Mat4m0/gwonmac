Click and double-click work as before again, Tools is restored, and small routine Guild Wars updates should no longer break GWonMac.

- **Mouse:** click and double-click behave as in the original game again.
- **Tools:** all GWonMac Tools work again.
- **Quick Travel:** lists every location, remembers your recent destinations, and lets you add your own.
- **Xunlai Chest:** opens only when your account has access to it.
- **Keyboard:** modifier keys, macOS text-editing shortcuts, and held keys in chat now behave as expected, and movement no longer continues after you release `⌘`.
- **Multiple Accounts:** accounts in the background keep running.
- **Future updates:** small routine Guild Wars updates should no longer affect your game or need an emergency GWonMac update. A larger update can still break something.
