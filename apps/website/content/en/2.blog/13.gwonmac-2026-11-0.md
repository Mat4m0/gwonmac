A new launcher for all your accounts, the Hub search in every game window, Switch Character, map guidance, and a built-in Guide. Your accounts, builds, and settings come with you.

- **A launcher for every account:** The launcher opens first. Choose **Play** for one account, or select several and open them together. **Accounts → Add account** creates another account with its own game window and sign-in. Your existing account is now **Main account**, with its login, builds, templates, and game files unchanged.
- **Hub:** Press <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>R</kbd> in a game window to search characters, accounts, travel destinations, builds, people, maps, and settings in one place. It works even with Tools off.
- **Switch Character:** Press <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>E</kbd> to change character from an outpost or the character screen.
- **Maps:** With Tools on, the :setting[Maps]{to="maps"} tool shows unexplored areas, walkable terrain, compass ranges, and an elite skill planner on the World Map.
- **More Tools:** Whispers (<kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>D</kbd>), Travel to friends and your Guild Hall, Quick Item Move, Effect Timers, Reminders, an Alcohol Timer, and Chat Filters. Each one is optional; turn them on in :setting[Tools]{to="tools"}.
- **Texture packs:** Import TexMod `.tpf` packs in :setting[Texture packs]{to="texture-packs"}.
- **Home and Help:** Home shows news and today's Zaishen, Shining Blade, Vanguard, and Nicholas Sandford rotations. Help has a Guide, the open issues in ArenaNet's client with what you can do, and a way to send us a message.

## What works differently now

- <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>R</kbd> opens the Hub. To reload a game window, press <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>Q</kbd> and choose **Reload Guild Wars**.
- Xunlai Storage opens with <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>S</kbd>. Shortcuts you changed yourself stay as they are.
- Settings is one page with sections for Tools, Maps, Appearance, Game, Texture packs, Game files, Updates, Home screen, and Advanced. Open it with <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>,</kbd> or the gear in the launcher.
- Build templates are now in the [Build Library]($docs/guides/templates). **Template files** there imports and exports Windows `.txt` templates.
