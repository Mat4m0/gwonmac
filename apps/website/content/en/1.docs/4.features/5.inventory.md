Two Tools save trips and clicks when you manage items: one opens your storage chest, the other moves stacks in one click.

## Xunlai Storage

Xunlai Storage opens your storage window without walking to the chest. It is the normal Guild Wars storage window, so you use it as usual.

- **Turn on:** :setting[Xunlai Storage]{to="tools/tool:xunlai-storage"}, needs :setting[Enable Tools]{to="tools/gwonmacTools"}. It is off by default.
- **Open:** <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>S</kbd>, **View → Open Xunlai Storage**, the chat command `/chest` or `/xunlai`, or search `storage` in the [Hub]($docs/features/hub). You can change the key next to Xunlai Storage in Settings → Tools.
- **Limits:** supported outposts only. If your character cannot reach storage where you stand, nothing opens and no error appears.

## Quick Item Move

Quick Item Move moves a whole stack with a click. With storage or a trade window open:

- <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>-click an item to move the whole stack.
- <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>Shift</kbd>-click an item to choose the amount in Guild Wars' own quantity box.

Where the stack goes depends on what is open:

- In your inventory with a trade open, the item goes into your trade offer. Otherwise it goes into storage.
- In storage, the item goes to your inventory.
- On your own trade offer, a click takes that offer back.
- The other player's offer is never changed.

A stack fills matching stacks first, then empty slots. If there is no room, the rest stays where it was.

- **Turn on:** :setting[Quick Item Move]{to="tools/tool:quick-item-move"}, needs :setting[Enable Tools]{to="tools/gwonmacTools"}. It is off by default.
- **Open:** it has no window. Open Xunlai Storage or a trade window, then Ctrl-click an item.
- **Limits:** it works only with an open storage or trade window. It stops working during PvP play.
