The Hub is one search box over the game. Type what you want, move with the arrow keys, and press Enter.

## Hub

The Hub finds the tools you have turned on, plus places, people, characters, saved builds and teams, accounts, settings, and calculations. The footer names what Enter will do. The header shows your character and where you are.

The game keeps running while the Hub is open. Press Escape to step back, and again to close it.

- **Turn on:** always on, even with Tools off. See :setting[Hub]{to="tools/hub"} in Settings → Tools.
- **Open:** <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>R</kbd> in a game window, or **View → Hub — Command Palette**. If you gave <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>R</kbd> to another feature, that feature wins and the menu item still opens the Hub.
- **Limits:** a tool that is off does not appear in search. While a map loads, at character selection, or in PvP, rows that cannot act stay in the list and say why.

### What you can search

- **Places:** outposts by name or shortcut (`kam`, `eotn`), your Guild Hall, and recent places. Needs :setting[Travel]{to="tools/tool:quick-travel"}.
- **Progress:** `progress`, or a goal word such as `vq` or `guardian`.
- **People:** friends by alias or character name. With :setting[Whispers]{to="tools/tool:whispers"} on, also your conversations and names seen in chat this session.
- **Characters:** type `char` and a name, or search a name directly.
- **Builds and teams:** `build` or `team` and a name. Needs Build Library.
- **Accounts:** `acc` and a name, or the **Switch Account** row. It opens or shows another account.
- **Trade:** `trade` and an item opens Trade Chat.
- **Calculations:** `1p in g`, `10e in p`, `2 * 250 + 75`. Enter copies the result. `titles` plans title points.
- **Settings:** type three or more letters of a setting or section, such as `memory` or `texture`. Enter opens that control.
- **Commands:** `settings`, `launcher`, `reload`, `resign`, and `commands`, which lists the search words and shortcuts.

### What you can do with a result

- **Enter** runs the action named in the footer. A result that changes the game or your account, such as travelling or switching character, only becomes selected when you click it. Press Enter, click the footer action, or double-click to run it.
- **Right arrow** opens a result's page, where it has one.
- <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>J</kbd> opens the **Actions** menu. There you can pin a result or give it an exact search phrase.
- <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>⌫</kbd> goes back one level from anywhere, including a text field.
- **Hub preferences** (search for it) reorders your pins and removes saved phrases.

### Prices and currency

Type an amount and `in` or `to`: `1 stack ecto in p`, `100k + 10e in a`. Gold and platinum convert offline. Items use trader quotes and recent Kamadan advertisements, and say when they were seen. A result that starts with `~` is inferred from advertised prices. Search `rates` to enter your own rates.

## Switch Character

Switch Character shows your characters as cards. Choose one and the game signs in to it without going through a logout. It also works from the character-selection screen.

Each card shows the profession pair, such as `Mo/Me`, and marks PvP characters. Start typing a name or primary profession to search.

- **Turn on:** :setting[Switch Character]{to="tools/tool:character-switch"}. It works without :setting[Enable Tools]{to="tools/gwonmacTools"}.
- **Open:** <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>E</kbd>, **View → Switch Character**, or search `char` in the Hub. Press Enter, click **Switch to …**, or double-click a card to switch.
- **Settings:** :setting[Show profession]{to="tools/characterSwitchProfession"}, :setting[Show level]{to="tools/characterSwitchLevel"}, :setting[Show location]{to="tools/characterSwitchLocation"}.
- **Limits:** in an explorable area it asks "Leave this area and switch to …?" first. While a map loads or in active PvP, it does not switch. The number keys 1 to 9 and 0 select one of the first ten characters but never switch. Names and search text are not saved.
