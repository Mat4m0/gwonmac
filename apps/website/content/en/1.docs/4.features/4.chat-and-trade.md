These tools sit beside Guild Wars' own chat. They add a whisper panel, a trade browser, quieter chat, and a key for calling targets.

## Whispers

Whispers is a movable panel for whisper conversations. It shows incoming whispers and the replies you send, one conversation per person. Guild Wars' own chat keeps working as before, and a reply you type there appears in the panel too.

A small movable icon shows unread messages without taking focus. Hide Whispers with the **Hide Whispers** button; your drafts stay until the session ends. Pick a person from friends, open conversations, recent people, and names seen in chat.

- **Turn on:** [Whispers](settings:tools/tool:whispers), needs [Enable Tools](settings:tools/gwonmacTools). It is off by default.
- **Open:** <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>D</kbd>, **View → Whispers**, or in the [Hub]($docs/features/hub) type `whisper` and a character name. Opening a result sends nothing; you press Enter or **Send**.
- **Settings:** in the panel's options menu: sound (**Off**, **When chat is in background**, or **Every incoming whisper**), **Background** opacity, **Mute this conversation**, **Close read conversations**, and **Clear recent people**.
- **Limits:** PvE only. Conversations last for this session only. They clear when you leave the game, change character, or turn Whispers off. The panel hides during loading screens and in PvP. A message has a length limit, which the message box shows. The panel holds up to 32 open conversations.

## Invite to party

In an outpost, type `invite` and the full character name in the Hub to invite that character. Only an exact name invites; a part of a name opens that person's actions instead. Type `invite ` with a space to list your online friends. Guild Wars answers in chat.

For a friend who is in another PvE outpost, **Travel and invite** travels there, then sends the invite once you arrive. If you change character first, or the trip does not arrive, no invite is sent.

- **Turn on:** [Whispers](settings:tools/tool:whispers). **Travel and invite** also needs [Travel](settings:tools/tool:quick-travel).
- **Open:** <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>R</kbd>, then `invite` and a name, or choose **Invite to party** on a person.
- **Limits:** PvE outposts only. **Travel and invite** is not offered for PvP outposts such as Random Arenas. If you land in a different district from your friend, Guild Wars cannot find them.

## Trade Chat

Trade Chat is a window for browsing recent Kamadan and Pre-Searing trade messages. Search by item or character name, and filter by **All**, **Selling**, or **Buying**. Select an offer to read it in full, copy the character name or the offer, or whisper the seller.

**Saved** keeps offers you starred and players you followed. **Trader prices** shows Kamadan NPC trader quotes with a short price history.

Trade Chat only reads. You post your own listings in Guild Wars, as before.

- **Turn on:** [Trade Chat](settings:tools/tool:trade-chat), needs [Enable Tools](settings:tools/gwonmacTools).
- **Open:** <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd>, **View → Trade Chat**, the chat command `/trade`, or in the Hub type `trade` and an item.
- **Limits:** works outside PvP. It needs a connection and reads a public trade feed, so it can show "Waiting for Kamadan to reconnect" with a **Try again** button. **Whisper** in an offer needs [Whispers](settings:tools/tool:whispers) on.

## Chat Filters

Chat Filters hides selected system notices before they enter your chat log.

- **Turn on:** [Chat Filters](settings:tools/tool:chat-filters), needs [Enable Tools](settings:tools/gwonmacTools).
- **Settings:** each option hides one kind of message when it is on: [Hide other party members' item drops](settings:tools/chatFilterAllyDrops), [Hide Hall of Heroes winner announcements](settings:tools/chatFilterHallOfHeroes), [Hide player title achievements](settings:tools/chatFilterTitleAchievements).
- **Limits:** works outside PvP.

## Call target

Call target calls the selected target without attacking it. It sends the Guild Wars shortcut <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>Shift</kbd> <kbd>Space</kbd> for you, from a key you choose.

- **Turn on:** [Call target](settings:tools/tool:call-target), needs [Enable Tools](settings:tools/gwonmacTools). It is off by default.
- **Open:** <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>G</kbd> while the game has keyboard focus. You can change or clear the key next to Call target in Settings → Tools. It has no Hub row and no menu item.
- **Limits:** it needs Guild Wars' default controls, because it sends that shortcut. It does nothing while you type in chat.
