The launcher is the window you see first. It lists your accounts, opens Guild Wars for each one, and holds the settings that do not belong inside a game window.

## Home

Home shows News and Dailies on two tabs. The bottom bar holds the account picker and the main button that opens your accounts.

Dailies lists today's activities, when they change, and a full-week table. The launcher works these out on your Mac, so Dailies also works without a connection.

- **Turn on:** :setting[News]{to="home/content.news"} and :setting[Dailies]{to="home/content.dailies"} in Settings → Home screen. Home removes the tab switcher when only one of them is on.
- **Open:** **Home** in the launcher's top bar.
- **Settings:** **News**, **Dailies**, **First Home tab** (shown when both are on). Under News: **Guild Wars updates**, **Upcoming events**, **GWonMac releases**, **Rotate featured news**.

## News

News gathers Guild Wars updates from the Guild Wars Wiki, seasonal events, and GWonMac release notes. Choose a story to read it in the launcher. **Open on Guild Wars Wiki** or **Open on the website** opens the full text in your browser.

- **Turn on:** :setting[News]{to="home/content.news"} in Settings → Home screen.
- **Open:** **News** in the launcher's top bar, or a story on Home. **All news** returns to the list.
- **Settings:** the news sources listed under Home. **Customize** on the News page opens them.
- **Limits:** Beta release notes appear only while the :setting[Beta update channel]{to="updates/updateTrack"} is selected.

## Accounts

The Accounts page lists every account with its state: **Ready**, **Open**, **Opening**, or a reason it failed. The count at the top reads, for example, "2 accounts · 1 open".

- **Open:** **Accounts** in the launcher's top bar, or **Accounts → Manage Accounts…** in the menu bar.

## Add account

**Add account** creates another account with its own Guild Wars window. Give it a name (up to 48 characters), and optionally an icon and a colour so you can tell accounts apart at a glance. Sign-in happens inside the game, not in the launcher.

- **Open:** **Add account** at the top of the Accounts page.
- **Limits:** you can add up to 16 accounts. Names must be different from each other.

## Play, Show, and opening several accounts

Each account has one game window. Use the buttons on its row to act on that account alone.

- **Play** opens a closed account.
- **Show** brings an open account's window to the front.
- **Try again** retries an account that failed to open.
- **Cancel** removes an account that is waiting for the game files.

To open several accounts together, tick their check boxes. The main button then reads **Open 2 accounts** (or the number you ticked). It opens them one after another. An account that is already open is never opened twice. When your only ticked account is already open, the main button reads **Show**. When several ticked accounts are all open, it reads **Accounts are open**.

When every ticked account has opened, the launcher hides and the first game window takes focus. If an account fails, the launcher stays visible so you can retry it.

- **Open:** the **Accounts** picker in the bottom bar of Home, or the rows on the Accounts page.
- **Limits:** at least one account stays ticked. Your ticked accounts are remembered for next time.

## Edit

**Edit** changes an account's name, icon, and colour. The new name also appears in the title of its game window.

- **Open:** **Edit** on the account's row.
- **Settings:** **Name**, **Icon**, **Color**. **Archive** hides an account without deleting its data. An archived account can be restored from **Archived accounts** at the bottom of the Accounts page.
- **Limits:** **Archive** is offered only for an account that is ready or failed, and never for the first account in the list.

## Main account

A fresh install starts with one account named **Main account**. If you used GWonMac before accounts were added, **Main account** keeps your existing saved login, builds, and game files in place.

You can rename **Main account**. It is the first account in the list and cannot be archived.

## What each account keeps, and what is shared

Each account keeps its own:

- sign-in and saved login (Guild Wars sign-in stays inside each game window);
- game storage;
- build library and templates, for an account you add;
- game window size and position.

All accounts share:

- the Guild Wars game files and their updates;
- GWonMac updates;
- every setting, including :setting[Tools and their shortcuts]{to="tools"};
- the :setting[texture pack]{to="texture-packs"} you choose.

Closing one game window affects only that account. Closing the last one shows the launcher again.

## Accounts menu

The **Accounts** menu in the menu bar lists your accounts while you play. Each entry shows its state: **Open**, **Waiting**, **Opening…**, or **Try again**. Choose an open account to show its window, or a ready one to open it. **Manage Accounts…** opens the Accounts page.

- **Open:** **Accounts** in the menu bar.

## Window → Show Launcher

When a game window has focus, the launcher may be hidden. **Show Launcher** brings it back without touching any game. It keeps the page you were on.

- **Open:** **Window → Show Launcher** in the menu bar, or right-click the Dock icon and choose **Show Launcher**. In the Hub, search `launcher`.

## Quit or Reload Game

<kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>Q</kbd> does not quit GWonMac straight away. It asks about the game window that has focus. **Reload Guild Wars** restarts that account with fresh memory. **Quit Game** closes it. **Cancel** changes nothing. Other accounts stay open.

Tick **Return to my character automatically** to sign back in to the same character after a reload. This is the same switch as :setting[Return to character after reload]{to="game/autoRelogAfterReload"} in Settings → Game.

Reload is the usual fix for black or corrupted textures and for a game that stutters after a long session. See [Known issues]($docs/guides/known-issues).

- **Open:** <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>Q</kbd>, **Quit or Reload Game…** in the app menu, **View → Reload Guild Wars…**, or search `reload` in the Hub.
- **Limits:** it acts on the game window that has focus, or on the only open game. With no game open, nothing happens.
