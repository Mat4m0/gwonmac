GWonMac installs like any other Mac app. You need an Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or newer) with macOS 12 Monterey or later, plus your Guild Wars account. The game itself is downloaded from ArenaNet on first launch.

## 1. Download

[Download the latest `.dmg`](/download){target="_blank"} and open the disk image. You can also get every release, with checksums and build attestations, from the [releases page](https://github.com/Mat4m0/gwonmac/releases/latest) on GitHub.

## 2. Move to Applications

Drag **Guild Wars** into the Applications folder shown in the disk image.

![The disk image window: drag Guild Wars Reforged onto the Applications folder](/docs/installation/dmg-install.webp)

## 3. Open and play

Open Guild Wars from Applications. The launcher checks the official client and downloads the files it needs. Quick Start lets you play while the remaining areas download in the background.

![The launcher downloading game files from ArenaNet, with the option to play while downloading](/docs/installation/download.webp)

Sign in with your Guild Wars account. Steam login works too.

![The Guild Wars login screen, running natively on macOS](/docs/installation/login.webp)

## Updating

Game data stays current on its own. When a new app version is out, the launcher shows a notice and installs it in one click. You can also check manually from the launcher.

## If something goes wrong

Most first-launch questions are covered in [Troubleshooting & known issues]($docs/guides/troubleshooting). For everything else, ask on [Discord](https://discord.gg/Z9ft52RBD3) or use the [GitHub bug form](https://github.com/Mat4m0/gwonmac/issues/new?template=bug-report.yml).
