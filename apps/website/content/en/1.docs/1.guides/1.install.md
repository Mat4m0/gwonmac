GWonMac installs like any other Mac app. You need an Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or newer) with macOS 12 Monterey or later, plus your Guild Wars account. The game itself is downloaded from ArenaNet on first launch.

## 1. Download

[Download the latest `.dmg`](/download){target="_blank"} and open the disk image. You can also get every release, with checksums and build attestations, from the [releases page](https://github.com/Mat4m0/gwonmac/releases/latest) on GitHub.

## 2. Move to Applications

Drag **Guild Wars Reforged** into the Applications folder shown in the disk image.

![The disk image window: drag Guild Wars Reforged onto the Applications folder](/docs/installation/dmg-install.webp)

## 3. Open and play

Open **Guild Wars Reforged** from Applications. On first launch the launcher welcomes you and offers the optional :setting[Tools]{to="tools/gwonmacTools"}; you can turn them on later in Settings. The launcher then downloads the official client. Choose **Play** as soon as it is ready; the remaining game data downloads in the background while you play.

Sign in inside the game window with your Guild Wars account. Steam login works too. If you have been playing on mobile with Sign in with Apple or Google, link that account first: [Accounts & mobile linking]($docs/guides/accounts).

![The Guild Wars login screen, running natively on macOS](/docs/installation/login.webp)

To play several accounts at once, choose **Accounts** in the launcher, then **Add account**. Each account opens its own game window and keeps its own sign-in.

## Updating

Game data stays current on its own. When a new app version is ready, the launcher shows **Launcher update ready**; choose **Restart and update**. You can also check yourself in :setting[Updates]{to="updates"} in Settings.

## If something goes wrong

Most first-launch questions are covered in [Troubleshooting]($docs/guides/troubleshooting) and [Known issues]($docs/guides/known-issues). For everything else, ask on [Discord](https://discord.gg/Z9ft52RBD3), [report a bug](https://github.com/Mat4m0/gwonmac/issues/new?template=bug-report.yml), or [request a feature](https://github.com/Mat4m0/gwonmac/issues/new?template=feature-request.yml).
