GWonMac runs on **every Apple Silicon Mac** with **macOS 12 Monterey or later**, from the M1 MacBook Air to the Mac Studio. Intel Macs are not supported.

## What testers report

These numbers come from public tester reports in the alpha and beta threads on r/GuildWars. Nobody ran a lab benchmark; this is what people measured on their own machines:

| Mac | Reported performance |
| --- | --- |
| MacBook Air M1 (8 GB) | Steady 60 FPS |
| MacBook Air M2 | 60 FPS at full 5K, max settings |
| MacBook Pro M1 Pro (16 GB) | Constant 60 FPS at 4K, highest graphics settings |
| MacBook Air M4 | Solid 60 FPS, controller supported |
| M5-class machines | 100–120 FPS at max settings |

Actual performance depends on your machine, display resolution, render scale, and graphics settings.

## Efficiency

On an M1 Pro, the alpha used about 70% of a single CPU core and around 500 MB of active memory during play, without any thermal warnings.

## Resolution and render scale

The app renders at native Retina resolution on internal and external displays. You can change the render scale (1x, 1.5x, 2x) in the app menu under **Guild Wars → Settings → Display**. The client's own graphics settings apply on top. If the picture looks soft, raise the render scale or turn off post-processing effects.

![The Display settings: graphics quality selectable as 1x, 1.5x, or Retina 2x, each with its render resolution](/docs/performance/render-scale.webp)

::note{icon="lucide:info"}
Antialiasing currently offers only "None". This comes from ArenaNet's WebAssembly client itself; the mobile version behaves the same way.
::

## Frame rate ceiling

Most testers see the client cap at 60 FPS on 60 Hz displays. Machines with higher-refresh displays have reported up to 120 FPS. GWonMac has no setting that unlocks frame rates beyond what the client and your display allow.

[Install GWonMac]($docs/guides/install) and see what your machine does. If your numbers differ from the table, please share them on [Discord](https://discord.gg/Z9ft52RBD3).
