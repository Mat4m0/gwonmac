GWonMac runs on **every Apple Silicon Mac** with **macOS 12 Monterey or later**, from the M1 MacBook Air to the Mac Studio. Intel Macs are not supported.

## What affects performance

GWonMac does not promise a fixed frame rate. Results vary with the Mac, display,
render scale, the client's graphics settings, the area being played, and the
current official client build. Community reports are useful feedback, but they
are not controlled benchmarks and are not used as performance guarantees.

## Render quality

The app renders at native Retina resolution on internal and external displays. Choose [Render quality](settings:game/renderScale) in Settings → Game: **Standard · 1×**, **High · 1.5×**, or **Very high · 2×**. The change applies at once. The client's own graphics settings apply on top. If the picture looks soft, raise the render quality or turn off post-processing effects in the game.

::note{icon="lucide:info"}
Antialiasing currently offers only "None". This comes from ArenaNet's WebAssembly client itself; the mobile version behaves the same way.
::

## Long sessions

ArenaNet's client can use at most 2 GB of memory, and it can close when it reaches that limit. [Extended memory](settings:game/extendedMemoryEnabled) lets it use up to 4 GB. It is experimental and applies after a restart. To see frame time and memory while you play, turn on the [Diagnostics overlay](settings:game/showDiagnostics).

## If play feels slow

Try a lower render quality first, then reduce the client's graphics settings. If the problem continues, [report it](#report) with a performance capture: choose **Help → Diagnostics → Start Performance Capture** in a game window, reproduce the problem, and stop the capture.
