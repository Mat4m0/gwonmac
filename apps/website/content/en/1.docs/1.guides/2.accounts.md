GWonMac runs ArenaNet's official client, so it uses ArenaNet's accounts. There is no GWonMac account or project login server. Credentials are sent only to the selected provider and ArenaNet's official client and services, never to a GWonMac service; if you enable saved login, GWonMac keeps them in this Mac's device-only Data Protection Keychain.

## Which logins work on Mac

| Login                                       | Works in GWonMac | Notes                                                     |
| ------------------------------------------- | ---------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| **ArenaNet account** (email and password)   | Yes              | The standard login.                                        |
| **Steam**                                   | Yes              | If your Guild Wars Reforged access came through Steam.     |
| **Mobile ID** (Sign in with Apple / Google) | Not directly     | Link it to an ArenaNet account first, described below.     |

Mobile ID exists only in the mobile client. The desktop client, the one GWonMac runs, asks for ArenaNet or Steam credentials, so a mobile-only account has nothing to type in.

## You play on iPad or Android and want to play on Mac

If you started on mobile with **Sign in with Apple** or **Sign in with Google**, your characters and any campaigns you bought in the mobile store live on a Mobile ID account. Link it to an ArenaNet account once, and that same account works on your Mac.

ArenaNet's steps, done on the phone or tablet you already play on:

1. Open Guild Wars Reforged on the mobile device.
2. At the character select screen, choose **Edit Account**.
3. Tap **Manage Account**, then the green **Next** button. The account management page opens.
4. Open the menu next to **Overview** in the top right and choose **Link Accounts**.
5. Enter the email and password of your ArenaNet account, then confirm with **Link Accounts**.

Afterwards you can sign in on mobile with either the Apple or Google account or your ArenaNet credentials, and sign in on your Mac with the ArenaNet credentials. The full instructions are in ArenaNet's support article, [Linking Your Guild Wars Reforged Mobile Account and ArenaNet Accounts](https://help.guildwars.com/hc/en-us/articles/52493031215123-Linking-Your-Guild-Wars-Reforged-Mobile-Account-and-ArenaNet-Accounts).

::note{icon="lucide:info"}
Linking asks for an ArenaNet account that already exists. If you have never created one, set it up at [account.arena.net](https://account.arena.net) before you start.
::

### Do campaigns bought on mobile carry over?

That is what linking is for. ArenaNet states the requirement for desktop play as a paid campaign plus a mobile account linked to an ArenaNet login, and mobile players buy campaigns through the in-game store on the mobile client. A campaign you already paid for there is that paid campaign; you do not buy it again for your Mac.

Purchases and account linking run entirely on ArenaNet's side. GWonMac has no access to either, so if something you bought does not show up after linking, ArenaNet support is the place to go.

## You already own Guild Wars on PC or Steam

Nothing to link. [Install GWonMac]($docs/guides/install), sign in with your ArenaNet or Steam credentials, and play. Going the other direction, choose **Sign In With Existing Account** on the mobile login screen and your characters and progress come with you.

## What a mobile-only account gives you

Before linking, a Mobile ID account is ad-supported and limited to the Prophecies campaign, with restrictions: no chat, no player-to-player trading, no PvP, and no sending party invitations, though other players can still invite you.

Those limits belong to the account type, not to the hardware. Sign in with an ArenaNet account that owns Guild Wars Reforged and they are gone, on mobile and on Mac alike.

## Where to get help

Linking, purchases, and login trouble are ArenaNet's territory. Submit a support ticket from the email address on your ArenaNet account, and include your mobile support ID.

If GWonMac itself misbehaves before you reach the login screen, see [Troubleshooting & known issues]($docs/guides/troubleshooting).
