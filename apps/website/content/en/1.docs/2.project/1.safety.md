The short answer: GWonMac is open source, runs ArenaNet's official client, and automates nothing. Whether that is allowed is a question only ArenaNet can answer. This page lays out what we know.

## What the app does

- It runs **ArenaNet's official WebAssembly client** inside a sandboxed macOS app. The game binary is unmodified, and no emulated Windows client is involved.
- Game files are **downloaded directly from ArenaNet** and verified before use. The app does not bundle or redistribute the game.
- It does **not automate gameplay**, send input on your behalf, or grant any advantage over other players.
- It does **not upload telemetry**, credentials, account identifiers, or game traffic. You sign in with your existing account (Steam login works) and the app never sees or stores your credentials.
- The source is public under [GPL-3.0](https://github.com/Mat4m0/gwonmac). Anyone can read exactly what it does.

## Is it a virus? How does macOS verify it?

Releases are signed with an Apple Developer ID, use Apple's hardened runtime, and are notarized and stapled before publication. macOS verifies every release through Gatekeeper when you open it. You never need to disable security settings. If a guide tells you to, you are not looking at an official release.

## Where ArenaNet stands

GWonMac is an independent, open-source project and is **not affiliated with or endorsed by ArenaNet or NCSoft**. It runs ArenaNet's official client, downloads official files directly from ArenaNet, and does not automate gameplay. ArenaNet has not published a policy that covers interoperability hosts like this one, and could take a different view in the future, for example if it ships its own Mac solution. Use it at your own discretion; only ArenaNet decides what its Terms of Service allow.

For context: ArenaNet's published add-on stance has historically focused on tools that grant unfair advantages. In May 2024 it explicitly permitted GWToolbox++ for PvE while reserving the right to change that determination. GWonMac works differently, since it hosts the client rather than hooking into it, but the same principle applies: ArenaNet's discretion is final.

## Will I get banned, then?

Nobody outside ArenaNet can promise you anything here, and this page won't pretend to. What we can say: the app's behaviour is fully inspectable, it changes nothing about gameplay, and the planned optional extras (GWonMac Tools) follow the standards ArenaNet set for permitted PvE tools: user-initiated only, disabled in all PvP.

If ArenaNet ever indicates that this approach is not permitted, the project will say so clearly and act accordingly.
